# MLB Spending vs. Team Performance ⚾📊

A data analytics project exploring the relationship between Major League Baseball team payrolls and on-field success during the 2025 MLB season.

---

## Project Overview

One of the biggest discussions in Major League Baseball is the lack of a salary cap and the large differences in team payrolls. This project investigates whether higher payroll spending actually translates to better performance.

Using payroll and team statistics data, I analyzed trends between:
- Team payroll
- Wins and losses
- Active vs Total cash
- Overall team success

The goal was to determine whether spending more money gives MLB teams a significant competitive advantage.

---

### Baseball Reference
Used for:

### Spotrac
Used for:

## Analysis Performed

The project includes:
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Merging payroll and performance datasets
- Correlation analysis
- Data visualization
- Statistical interpretation

Visualizations were created to help identify trends between payroll spending and team performance.

---

## Key Findings

- Higher payroll teams generally performed better overall.
- Some lower-payroll teams still outperformed expectations.
- Payroll alone does not guarantee success.
- Team management and efficiency still play major roles in MLB performance.
